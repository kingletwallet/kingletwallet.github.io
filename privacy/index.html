Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Kinglet Wallet does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your wallet, your addresses or your transactions.

Who we are

Kinglet Wallet is published by Neralo AB. Contact: support@kingletwallet.com.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your wallet description (the public descriptor or extended public key you import). Stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only. It is not synced to iCloud.
  • Wallet history (balances, addresses, transactions). Stored in a local database on this device and excluded from device backups.
  • Notes you write on transactions and coins. Stored on this device only.
  • Server settings (the address of your server and its certificate). Stored in the app's settings on this device.
  • App preferences (such as the unit you show amounts in). Stored in the app's settings and included in normal device backups. They contain no wallet data.

Your wallets, history and notes are excluded from iCloud and computer backups, and your wallet descriptions stay in this device's Keychain. Nothing about your wallets leaves the phone through a backup. On a new or restored phone, you import your wallets again.

Kinglet Wallet is watch-only. It never holds private keys. Signing happens on a separate signer.

Deleting the app or using Remove wallet erases this data. We have no copy.

Export and import

You can export your notes (as a BIP-329 file) and your transaction history (as CSV). Exports go through the iOS share sheet, and you choose where they go. You can import notes and a server certificate through the iOS file picker. The app never sends these files anywhere on its own. Exported files are plain text, so anyone who gets them can read them.

Your server

The app connects only to the Electrum server you choose, typically one you run yourself. To show your balance, the app asks that server about your addresses and sends it the transactions you approve. That server is operated by you or by someone you choose, not by us, and its own policies apply.

The connection is encrypted with TLS, and the app remembers your server's certificate. Unencrypted connections are only allowed to a .onion address over Tor. If you reach your server over Tor, the traffic passes through Tor relays via a separate Tor app, such as Orbot. That app's own privacy policy applies.

Fee suggestions and checks on your transactions also come from your own server. The demo wallet never connects anywhere.

Links out of the app

Under Help › Licenses, the Source code links open github.com in your browser when you tap them. That is the only way the app leads to a third party, and only when you tap. GitHub's privacy policy applies there.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan QR codes (wallet import and signed transactions). Images are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Local network: used only to reach your server on your own network.
  • Clipboard: read only when you tap Paste.

Apple

You download the app from the App Store under Apple's terms. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. Apple's privacy policy covers that.

This website

kingletwallet.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes; see the GitHub Pages data collection notice. We do not receive those logs.

If you email us, we keep your message and address only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

The app gives us no personal data about you, so there is nothing there for us to access, correct or delete. You can contact us with any question, and you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, for example by offering a server we run, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
