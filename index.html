Self-custody for Bitcoin XBT

Your keys never touch your phone.

Kinglet is a wallet coordinator for Bitcoin XBT on the BLAKE2b chain. It shows your balances and builds your transactions. Your air-gapped signer signs them, and Kinglet sends.

How it works What you need

Pay from your phone.
Sign offline.

A phone is online all day and runs many other apps, which makes it a risky place for keys.

Keys stay on your signer

Even someone with your phone cannot move your coins without your signer.

No account

No sign-up, no email, no phone number. No analytics and no tracking. We never see your wallet.

Your own server

Kinglet talks only to the Electrum server you choose. No third-party services.

How it works

Three steps.

1

Import a watch-only wallet

Export your wallet from your signer and scan it with Kinglet.

2

Prepare and review

Choose the amount, the fee and, if you like, which coins to spend. Kinglet shows what you pay before anything is signed.

3

Sign air-gapped, then send

Your signer scans a QR code from Kinglet and you approve. Kinglet scans the signed transaction back and sends it.

Replay protection

Safe from replay on the SHA256 chain.

Coins from before the fork exist on both chains. A transaction signed the old way could be copied to the SHA256 chain and spend your coins there too.

Unified sighash

Kinglet asks your signer for SIGHASH_UNIFIED (0x21). The rules on the SHA256 chain reject that hash type, so the transaction cannot be replayed there.

Every signature checked

When the signed transaction comes back, Kinglet checks each signature. If any signature uses SIGHASH_ALL (0x01) instead, Kinglet refuses to send the transaction.

Follows the BLAKE2b chain

Kinglet checks that your server follows the BLAKE2b chain, so your balance and history come from the right side of the fork.

What you need.

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later

Kinglet is free, with no in-app purchases.

An air-gapped signer

One that signs with unified sighash (0x21) and exchanges transactions as animated QR codes (UR or BBQr), such as SeedSigner or Coldcard Q.

An Electrum server

On a BLAKE2b node, for example Fulcrum on StartOS or Electrs on Umbrel. Setup options

Want to try it first? The demo wallet works without a signer or a server.
